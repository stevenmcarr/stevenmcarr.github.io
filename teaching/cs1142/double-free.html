Double free, unnoticed

Freeing a dangling pointer after its block has been handed out again frees someone else's data, and glibc cannot tell. Step through the slide 14 program, or switch to Live heap and run your own malloc, free and strcpy. Simplified glibc model on x86-64: 8-byte header, block sizes a multiple of 16, minimum 32, one free list per size.
