Dr. Carr served as Graduate Program Director at Michigan Tech from 1996–2003 and 2006–2010, and as undergraduate adviser from 2000–2009. At WMU he has mentored six faculty members in the Department of Computer Science through to tenure.

Curriculum development

Much of his NSF-funded work has been curricular, aimed at teaching topics that students typically find abstract — concurrency, access control, memory safety — through tools that let them see the behavior directly. That work is described in more detail on the research page, and the resulting papers are collected under publications.

Degree programs he has helped design, launch, or substantially revise:

  • M.S. in Intelligent Systems and Data Engineering, with the Department of Industrial Engineering
  • B.S. and M.S. in Cybersecurity, with the Department of Business Information Systems
