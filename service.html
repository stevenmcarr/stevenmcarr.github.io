Key administrative initiatives

Research and graduate education

  • Proposed a more accurate method of accounting for internal research expenditures. It was implemented by the current VP for Research and has helped WMU become an emerging R1 institution.
  • Designed a simplified administrator incentive program for administrators holding research funding.
  • Reorganized the Technology and Commercialization Unit after major budget cuts and staff loss.
  • Helped shepherd policies on intellectual property, conflict of interest, and research misconduct through the WMU Faculty Senate.
  • Helped lead development of a proposal to establish a funded undergraduate research program in the College of Engineering and Applied Sciences.
  • Developed an internal graduate application processing app for the college to streamline application processing.
  • Created opportunity for faculty to increase research proposals and expenditures through curriculum reorganization and a course buyout structure.

Programs and partnerships

  • Helped develop a proposal for a STEM workforce development program funded by the U.S. Department of Education.
  • Helped develop a proposal for a multi-million dollar bequest to WMU that includes an endowed professorship in the Department of Computer Science.
  • Worked with Iraqi government representatives to bring 25 faculty from Iraqi universities to the Department of Computer Science to earn a Ph.D. in computer science.
  • Conducted the first personal visits from a chair to Department of Computer Science alumni in Silicon Valley and Washington, D.C.
  • Helping to develop a Silicon Valley Industrial Advisory Board for the Department of Computer Science.

Faculty

  • Led the hiring of the department’s first NSF CAREER award and first NIH award recipient.
  • Successfully mentored six faculty members in the Department of Computer Science at WMU to tenure.

Degree-program development and accreditation work is listed on the teaching page.
